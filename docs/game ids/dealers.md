# Dealerships and used cars

Generated by `scripts/data/dump_dealers.py` from the Simulation disc (USA). `dealers.json` holds the same data, with the price and power gain of each power part.

- **Dealers** are the manufacturer IDs of the dealerships whose pages have a tile for the car (kind 6 elements, see `SimMap_Update`). **New** is its price there (`Car_GetNewPrice`, car list `+0x44`).
- **Used** is the lowest price over the 60 periods of `.usedcar_usa` and **Periods** how many periods list the car. The lot changes every 10 days (`UsedCar_LoadPeriod`). Only the Japanese manufacturers have a used car lot.
- **Power** is the stock power (see `cars.md`). In the JSON, `powerGains` is the power each part adds when it is the only part installed (the racing modification with weight reduction 3, which it needs). Gains don't add up exactly.
